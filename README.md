# Student-Dropout-Prediction
Introduction 
The aim of this project is to predict the academic outcomes of students by using machine 
learning algorithms. There has been a big rise in decision making done through the usage 
of data. This is due to the usage of Artificial Learning (AI). Artificial Intelligence has 
revolutionised numerous domains. This includes the domain of education. For the domain 
of education, large data sets can be analysed in order to get insights, predict future trends 
and make strategic decisions. The aim of this project is to successfully predict student 
dropouts using previous data. This will help the educational institutions to provide support 
to students who are at a risk of dropping out. 
This project explores the problem domain of Student dropout. Student dropout is a huge 
socioeconomic issue around the world. There can be various reasons as to why a student 
may choose to dropout and quit studies. These reasons may include financial issues, 
mental health issues or motivation or life changes. Issues students can have include cost 
of tuition, cost of additional expenses and cost of living. Issues that students may face 
related to their mental wellbeing/ mental health include Depression, Anxiety and 
Substance Abuse, All of these reasons have the ability to lead a student to dropping out.

This survey showed that 32 percent of the students dropped out of the college due to 
personal or family issues, 24 percent of the students dropped out due to financial issues, 
11 percent of the students dropped out due to wanting to pursue another career, 10 
percent of the students dropped out because they were dissatisfied with the instituition 
and so on.  
Ensuring that students finish their education is known to be very important for their futures. 
Student dropout has the potential to negatively impact the lives of the students and the 
institutions where the students are enrolled. For students, it can limit their future 
employment opportunities and wellbeing. For educational institutions, high dropout rates 
can appear poorly on their records and affect their reputation. On a societal level, high 
dropout rates can hinder economic growth.  
Because this is such a big challenge it is no surprise that research regarding student 
dropout has been going on for several decades. Early research on this topic datesas far 
as the 1970s (Tinto, 1975). As time has passed, this research has expanded and the use 
of modern technologies , computing power and data collection techniques has further 
enhanced the ability to study the problem more accurately. 
The primary aim of this project is to develop and assess machine learning models to 
forecast student dropout based on historical data. This is to help educational institutions 
to take early measures. 
The objectives of this project are outlined below:  
➢ To compare three machine learning algorithms for accuracy and effectiveness based on 
various relevant metrics. 
➢ To evaluate the effectiveness of all of the three models using performance metrics.  
➢ To successfully identify students at risk for dropping out using machine learning models.
